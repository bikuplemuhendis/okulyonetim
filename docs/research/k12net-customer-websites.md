# K12NET Customer / School Website Inventory

Research date: 2026-09-03

## 1. Methodology

This inventory was assembled through open-web research only (no application code). Sources and techniques:

- **Vendor reference logos**: Scraped K12NET’s public references page (`https://k12net.com/atlas-yazilim/referanslarimiz/`, Sept 2025 snapshot cited by vendor as 4,358 active institutions). Logo filenames often embed the customer subdomain (e.g. `erciyeskoleji.k12net.com`).
- **Customer testimonials**: K12NET Turkish homepage and `https://k12net.com/musteri-yorumlari/` name specific schools.
- **Targeted web searches**: Queries including `"k12net" veli girişi`, `"k12net.com"`, `site:k12.tr k12net`, school parent handbooks/PDFs, and international school portal pages.
- **School website verification**: Where possible, confirmed customers have a K12NET portal URL or explicit K12NET mention on the school’s own domain.
- **Confidence levels**:
  - **Confirmed**: Portal URL or explicit K12NET usage documented on the school’s official site, OR named K12NET customer testimonial plus matching reference-logo subdomain.
  - **Likely**: Listed on K12NET reference logos (active-customer marketing list) without independent school-site verification in this pass.

**Limitations**: K12NET does not publish a single downloadable customer list with official websites. Subdomain→school name mapping for reference logos is inferred from filename slugs and may include course centers, branches, or stale entries. Ministry-scale deployments (UAE ~1,000 schools, Qatar ~400 schools per K12NET) are noted regionally but individual school URLs are not enumerated here.
